Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Multi-Relay Bot is a private tool run by one person for their own accounts. It does not access anyone else's data.

What it accesses

  • Google / YouTube: one permission, youtube.upload, solely to upload videos to the operator's own channel.
  • Facebook and Instagram: permission to publish videos to the operator's own Page and Instagram account.
  • X: permission to post to the operator's own X account.
  • TikTok: user.info.basic (to read the account's posting limits) and video.publish (to post videos to the operator's own TikTok account).

It does not read followers, messages, comments or any other account data.

What it stores

The access tokens and a list of already-relayed video IDs, kept privately on the operator's own server. No analytics, no advertising, no data sold or shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in each platform's settings, for example myaccount.google.com/permissions, or in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
