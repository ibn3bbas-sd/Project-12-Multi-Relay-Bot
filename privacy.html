Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

YouTube Relay Bot is a private tool run by one person for their own YouTube channel.

What it accesses

It requests one Google permission, youtube.upload, solely to upload videos to the channel of the account that authorized it. It does not read, store or share any other Google account data, and it does not access other users' data.

What it stores

The OAuth token and a list of already-relayed video IDs, kept privately on the operator's own server. No analytics, no advertising, no data sold or shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/ibn3bbas-sd/Project-12-YouTube-Relay-Bot/issues.
